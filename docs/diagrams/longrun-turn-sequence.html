Sequence · Longrun v0.7.2 · one chat turn · one tool call · one persist

What happens between POST /api/chat and the SSE frames on the WebUI.

A single user message becomes a turn: assemble_turn composes the prompt, the provider streams tokens, the loop may emit a tool call that is looked up in the registry and gated by an approval queue + a real sandbox, keepgoing decides whether to loop or stop, and every step is published to /api/feed and persisted to flat atomic JSON stores. Approval branching and the keepgoing loop are shown as combined fragments, not free-floating arrows.
